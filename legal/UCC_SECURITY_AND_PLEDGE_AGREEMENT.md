# COMMERCIAL SECURITY AND PLEDGE AGREEMENT (UCC ARTICLE 9)

This Security and Pledge Agreement is dated as of [Date], by and between **[Mining Operating Company]**, as Debtor ("Debtor"), and **DGX Mine Streaming Capital Corp**, as Secured Party ("Secured Party").

---

### 1. Grant of Security Interest
To secure prompt payment and physical delivery of all gold under the Master Forward Gold Purchase and Streaming Agreement, Debtor hereby grants to Secured Party a continuing, first-priority security interest in all right, title, and interest of Debtor in and to the following property (the "Collateral"):
(a) All minerals, gold ore, doré, concentrates, precipitates, and refined bullion now existing or hereafter extracted from the Concession;
(b) All warehouse receipts, bills of lading, assay certificates, and depository receipts;
(c) All refining contracts, tripartite escrow accounts, and commercial bank accounts holding proceeds of mineral sales;
(d) All books, records, geological drill core libraries, and NI 43-101 / SEC S-K 1300 technical data.

### 2. Financing Statements
Debtor authorizes Secured Party to file UCC-1 Financing Statements in all relevant state and international jurisdictions (including Nevada Secretary of State, Wyoming Secretary of State, and Ontario Personal Property Security Registry).

### 3. Representations & Warranties
Debtor warrants that it has sole and unencumbered title to the Collateral and that no prior liens exist on the mineral production or doré off-take.

### 4. Remedies Upon Default
Upon default, Secured Party may:
(a) Notify refineries to redirect all deliveries of refined bullion directly to Secured Party;
(b) Enter upon the Concession premises to take possession of stockpiles and processing facilities;
(c) Exercise all remedies of a secured creditor under the Uniform Commercial Code.
