---

### 1. Scope of Services
DGX shall provide the following professional advisory services:
(a) **Geological & Regulatory Review**: Assessment of Company's mineral inventory under Canadian NI 43-101 and SEC Regulation S-K subpart 1300.
(b) **Streaming Structuring**: Formulation of definitive forward production purchase contracts, lien structures under UCC Article 9, and tripartite refinery escrows.
(c) **Sovereign L1 Tokenization**: Deployment of permissioned smart contracts (`DGXBullionToken.sol` and `MiningStreamingForge.sol`) representing allocated physical bullion and streaming rights.
(d) **Capital Placement & Syndication**: Introduction and syndication of the Transaction to institutional private credit desks, family offices, sovereign wealth funds, and digital asset liquidity providers.

### 2. Compensation & Retainer
* **Retainer Fee**: A non-refundable cash retainer of One Hundred Thousand Dollars ($100,000 USD), payable upon execution.
* **Structuring & Placement Fee**: A success fee equal to three percent (3.0%) of aggregate gross capital advanced under any streaming facility or debt instrument.
* **Tokenized Carried Interest**: Two percent (2.0%) of the minted forward stream tokens on the DGX Sovereign L1.

### 3. Exclusivity
For a period of twelve (12) months from execution, the Company agrees that DGX shall act as exclusive advisor for all precious metals streaming, asset tokenization, and sovereign blockchain capitalization.

### 4. Indemnification & Limitation
The Company agrees to indemnify and hold harmless DGX, its affiliates, directors, and agents from any liabilities or claims arising out of the Company's disclosures or technical reports.
