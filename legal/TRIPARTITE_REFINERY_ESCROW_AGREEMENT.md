# TRIPARTITE REFINERY ESCROW & SETTLEMENT AGREEMENT

This Tripartite Agreement is entered into among:

1. **OPERATOR**: [Mining Operating Company] ("Mine Operator");
2. **PURCHASER / TRUSTEE**: DGX Mine Streaming Capital Corp / DGX Gold Statutory Trust ("DGX"); and
3. **ACCREDITED REFINERY**: [Valcambi SA / PAMP SA / Argor-Heraeus SA] ("Refinery").

---

### 1. Purpose & Precedence
Refinery acknowledges that Operator has granted to DGX a first-priority lien on all unrefined gold doré produced from the Concession, and that DGX has the exclusive legal right to receive the Committed Stream Percentage of refined bullion.

### 2. Doré Intake & Assay Protocol
* Upon receipt of armored shipments of unrefined doré from the Concession, Refinery shall execute independent double fire assays to establish fine troy ounces to 0.0001 precision.
* Refinery shall deliver copies of all assay certificates simultaneously to Operator and DGX.

### 3. Segregation & Good Delivery Casting
* Refinery shall smelt, refine, and cast the Committed Stream Percentage into LBMA Good Delivery 400 oz gold bars (minimum 99.99% fineness).
* Refinery shall stamp each bar with refiner mark, unique serial number, fineness, and year of casting.

### 4. Direct Transfer to DGX Depository Vault
* Refinery is irrevocably instructed by Operator to release and transfer the refined Good Delivery bars directly to the **DGX Vault Custody SPV account** at Zurich Freezone or London LBMA vaults.
* Under no circumstances shall Refinery release physical bars or net dollar proceeds to Operator until DGX issues a cryptographic and written Release Certificate.
