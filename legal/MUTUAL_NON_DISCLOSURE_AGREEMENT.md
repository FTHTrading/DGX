# INSTITUTIONAL MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of the Effective Date by and between:

**PARTY A**: DGX Global Gold Network / DGX Mining Streaming Capital Corp, a corporate entity organized under the laws of the State of Wyoming, USA, having its principal executive offices at 30 N Gould St, Ste R, Sheridan, WY 82801 ("DGX"); and

**PARTY B**: The undersigned mining operator, institutional asset manager, or commercial participant identified on the signature page hereto ("Counterparty").

DGX and Counterparty are individually referred to as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties."

---

### 1. Purpose
The Parties wish to explore a potential business relationship involving the evaluation, underwriting, and structuring of in-ground mining asset tokenization, forward gold production purchase facilities, regulatory filings under SEC Regulation S-K 1300 / NI 43-101, and sovereign blockchain settlement rails (the "Purpose").

### 2. Definition of Confidential Information
"Confidential Information" means all non-public, proprietary, technical, financial, geological, legal, or commercial information disclosed by one Party (the "Disclosing Party") to the other Party (the "Receiving Party"), whether orally, visually, in writing, or in electronic format. Confidential Information specifically includes, but is not limited to:
(a) Geological assays, drill hole logs, metallurgical recovery curves, mine plans, feasibility studies, and NI 43-101 / SEC S-K 1300 technical report summaries;
(b) Streaming pricing formulas, royalty percentages, buyback options, and CAPEX disbursement schedules;
(c) Post-quantum Dilithium-3 key architectures, Proof-of-Gold consensus specifications, and smart contract source code;
(d) Depository vault coordinates, Lloyd's of London Specie insurance policies, and security escort protocols.

### 3. Obligations of Receiving Party
The Receiving Party agrees:
(a) To hold the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information in strict confidence, exercising at least the same degree of care it uses for its own confidential information of like nature, but no less than a reasonable degree of care;
(b) Not to disclose Confidential Information to any third party, except to its officers, directors, attorneys, accountants, technical Qualified Persons, and financial advisors who need to know such information for the Purpose and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as restrictive as this Agreement;
(c) Not to use Confidential Information for any purpose other than evaluating and executing the business transaction contemplated herein.

### 4. Non-Circumvention
Counterparty expressly covenants that it shall not, directly or indirectly, circumvent, avoid, bypass, or obviate DGX's relationships with depositories (Zurich Freezone, LBMA vaults), refineries (Valcambi, PAMP, Argor-Heraeus), insurance syndicates (Lloyd's of London), or institutional capital syndicates disclosed in connection with this evaluation.

### 5. Term & Termination
This Agreement shall remain in effect for a period of two (2) years from the Effective Date. The confidentiality obligations regarding trade secrets, geological reserve proprietary data, and cryptographic source code shall survive indefinitely.

### 6. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution
This Agreement shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, the laws of the State of Delaware, USA, without regard to conflicts of law principles. Any dispute arising hereunder shall be submitted to confidential, binding arbitration administered by JAMS in Wilmington, Delaware, under its Comprehensive Arbitration Rules.
