### Detailed Breakdown of Insurance Layers

#### Layer 1: Mine-Level Policies (In-Ground Through Refinery Doorstep)
Prior to physical casting into LBMA Good Delivery bars, gold is exposed to operational, extraction, and transit hazards. Rather than requiring token holders or the DGX treasury to purchase duplicative mining insurance, DGX mandates and audits the mine operator's existing enterprise policies:
* **Commercial Property & Casualty**: Covers crushing facilities, leaching circuits, and unrefined stockpiles against fire, equipment failure, and structural collapse.
* **Inland Marine & Transit Insurance**: Full-value insurance covering doré transit from the remote mine site to the accredited refinery, underwritten by tier-1 global carriers (e.g. Travelers, Chubb, Zurich Insurance Group).
* **Armored Carrier Bailee Coverage**: Carrier liability policies provided by Brink's Global Services or Loomis International during intercontinental transit.
* **DGX as Additional Loss Payee**: DGX Mine Streaming Capital Corp is formally endorsed as an **Additional Insured and Primary Loss Payee** on all operator transit certificates.

#### Layer 2: Depository-Level Master Policy (Refinery Doorstep Through Vault Bin)
Once doré is smelted and cast into LBMA 400 oz Good Delivery bars:
* **$1,000,000,000 Master Specie Coverage**: Primary coverage underwritten by leading syndicates at **Lloyd's of London** (including Beazley, Hiscox, and Atrium).
* **Covered Perils**:
  * Physical loss or damage to gold bullion;
  * Armed robbery, theft, and mysterious disappearance;

  * Catastrophic structural casualty.
* **Sovereign Freezone Advantage**: Storing bars in the **Zurich Freezone (Switzerland)** ensures assets are held outside the traditional SWIFT banking perimeter, protected by Swiss federal bonded warehouse statutes.
