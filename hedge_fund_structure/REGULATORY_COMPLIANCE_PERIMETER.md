# DGX GLOBAL COMPLIANCE & REGULATORY PERIMETER

## 1. Multi-Jurisdictional Architecture

### United States (SEC & CFTC)
* **Primary Capital Formation**: Structured under **SEC Regulation D Rule 506(c)** for verified accredited investors and institutional allocators, with parallel **Regulation S** for non-US investors.
* **Commodity Classification**: Physical DGX Bullion Tokens represent digital warehouse receipts for physical commodities (CFTC jurisdiction), exempt from definition of a security when paired with 100% physical delivery rights.
* **Streaming Investment Notes**: Issued via corporate debt/royalty vehicles to institutional partners.

### European Union & Switzerland (FinMA & MiCA)
* **FinMA Classification**: Structured as Asset-Backed Payment Token backed 100% by physical gold in Swiss Freezones.
* **MiCA Compliance**: Asset-Referenced Token (ART) provisions with full 1:1 reserve segregation and mandatory statutory liquidity buffers.

### Cross-Border Standards
* **ISO 20022 Messaging**: Full messaging format compatibility for institutional cross-border settlements via SWIFT alternative networks.
* **KYC/AML Standards**: Real-time integration with institutional compliance providers (Chainalysis, TRM Labs, Elliptic) enforcing wallet whitelist gates.
