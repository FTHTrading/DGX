# DGX MINING FORWARD STREAMING FACILITY // UNDERWRITING GUIDELINES

## 1. Investment & Underwriting Mandate
The DGX Mining Streaming Facility provides non-dilutive growth capital to mid-tier and development-stage gold mining operators.

### Investment Parameters:
* **Target Ticket Size**: $10,000,000 to $50,000,000 per mining operation.
* **Reserve Standard Required**: NI 43-101 (Canada/SEC S-K 1300) or JORC Code (Australia/South Africa).
* **Minimum Reserve Coverage**: Proven & Probable (P&P) reserves must equal at least 2.5x the total committed streaming volume over the contract term.
* **Delivery Price**: Fixed purchase price of $750 to $950 per fine troy ounce upon physical delivery to designated refinery, or 30% of prevailing London PM fix.
* **Cash-Flow Margin**: Generating $1,700 to $1,900+ per ounce in net margin at $2,650 spot gold.

## 2. Security & Lien Structure
* **First-Priority Commercial Lien**: UCC-1 financing statements filed against production permits, mill facilities, and unrefined doré stockpiles.
* **Refinery Escrow**: Tri-party refinery agreement obligating accredited refineries (Valcambi, PAMP, Metalor) to deliver refined bullion directly to DGX Depository Vaults prior to settling residual cash to the operator.
* **ESG Compliance Covenants**: Mine operators must adhere to the World Gold Council's Responsible Gold Mining Principles (RGMPs) and integrate IoT emission monitoring.
